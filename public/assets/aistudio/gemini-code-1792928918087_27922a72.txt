=== 3. PLAYER (route /watch/:id) ===
Layout: `100dvh`, black, `object-fit: contain`, custom DOM controls, native controls disabled, `playsInline`, `preload="metadata"`. Fullscreen the player CONTAINER; on iPhone fall back to `video.webkitEnterFullscreen()`.

CRITICAL: NEVER put `currentTime` in React state. Updating it 60 times a second would re-render the whole player tree and cause severe lag. Update the seek bar fill, buffered ranges and time labels through refs and `requestAnimationFrame` direct DOM writes. React state is only for coarse events (playing, paused, buffering, error, panel open).

Controls:
- Seek bar: thin, grows on hover/drag; buffered ranges, accent progress, draggable thumb. Pointer Events with `setPointerCapture`.
- Left: Play/Pause, Back 10s, Forward 10s, Volume (icon expands to slider on hover, mute toggle, persisted).
- Top-left: back arrow + title.
- Right: Next Episode, Episodes panel, Subtitles & Audio panel, Speed, Quality, PiP, Fullscreen.

Behavior:
- Controls auto-hide after 3000 ms of inactivity.
- Touch: single tap toggles controls after 250 ms delay; double-tap on left/right third seeks -10s/+10s with a ripple; center tap plays/pauses.
- Keyboard: Space/K play-pause, J/Left -10s, L/Right +10s, Up/Down volume, M mute, F fullscreen, C captions, P PiP, N next episode. Brief `aria-live="polite"` toast for each action.
- Smart Resume: save position every 5s (throttled) and on `pause`, `pagehide`, `visibilitychange`. 
- Resiliency: buffering spinner only after 400 ms stall. Clean error state with Retry. Automatically downgrade quality on repeated chunk failures.
